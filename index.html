What you can do

Basic covers everyday cleaning. Pro adds merge, compare, templates, PDF, reports, and more.

Remove duplicates

Exact matches and similar names (“Juan Perez” / “Juan Pérez”). You choose which columns to compare.

Compare two Excel files

See what was added, removed, or changed. Useful for audits and monthly versions.

Merge files

Combine several workbooks into one instead of copy-paste from every branch or month.

Broken formulas

Find #REF!, #N/A, #VALUE! and similar errors in a report. CleanSheet lists them so you can fix them in Excel — it does not rewrite formulas automatically.

Automatic reports

Totals, charts, and a summary from the sheet you already have — without building the formulas by hand.

Formula builder

Describe the calculation in Spanish (the way Excel is used in Latin America). CleanSheet gives you the formula ready to paste, including functions such as SUMAR.SI.CONJUNTO.

Paste from WhatsApp

Turn a copied chat list into a table, then save as Excel.

Reports from your own data

FAQ

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once per plan. The license is yours to keep for that version line.

Does it work offline?

Yes for cleaning. You only need internet when you first activate the Lemon key.

What language is the app?

Spanish and English. Switch in the toolbar. Date and text tools are built for Latin American Excel (day first, Spanish months).

Will it change my original file?

No. CleanSheet always writes a new file.

Mac or phone?

Not yet. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, one .exe. No need to install Python.

Who is it for?

Accountants, admins, sales teams, and small businesses that live in Excel — in Guatemala, Mexico, the rest of LATAM, and anywhere English is easier.
